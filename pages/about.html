ABOUT RAFIKI ZESPÓŁ

More Than Music. A Movement With Purpose.

We are a gospel worship movement raising a generation to know Christ, discover purpose and serve God's purpose in their own generation.

OUR STORY

A sound with a purpose.

Rafiki Zespół is a gospel worship movement built around a simple but powerful conviction: our generation has a purpose to serve.

We believe music is more than sound and worship is more than a moment. Through music, fellowship, ministry and community impact, we seek to create spaces where people can encounter God, grow in faith and discover who they were created to be.

What we carry is not limited to a stage. It extends into relationships, churches, communities and the everyday lives of young people who are searching for meaning and direction.

Rafiki means friendship, and that identity is important to us. We desire to build genuine relationships, walk together and grow together as we pursue God's purpose.

OUR FOUNDATION

“He served God's purpose in his own generation.”

Acts 13:36

This is more than a Bible verse to us. It is a reminder of the responsibility we carry.

We want to use the gifts God has placed in us — our music, our creativity, our relationships, our time and our resources — to serve His purpose in the generation He has placed us in.
